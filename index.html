The problem

The record is scattered, so it leaves when people do.

Coaches change, programmes end, families move. Each is normal, and each erases years of development when the history lives in six places.

WhatsApp

Session notes and results buried in a group chat.

Paper

A register in a bag, a notebook that stays with one coach.

Spreadsheets

One per coach, one per season, none that agree.

Coach turnover

What a coach knew is gone the day they move on.

Athlete movement

A new club or school starts again from nothing.

Lost history

In five years nobody can say where a number came from.

How Athlytica works

Record it once. Carry it forward.

Record→ Organise→ Review→ Publish→ Carry history forward

Record

Sessions and attendance, captured at the time by a named coach.

Organise

Assessments against a versioned protocol, kept with the athlete under their organisation.

Review

The coach writes a Development Review from the recorded evidence.

Publish

A review reaches the family only when the coach chooses to publish it.

Carry forward

The history stays with the athlete. A new organisation still needs its own access.

Continuity is not permission: keeping a record connected does not give anyone access to it.

The Development Review

A coach's judgement, written down with its evidence.

Coach-authored.
Evidence-linked.
Not independently verified.

  • Coach-authored — written by an identified coach, under their organisation.
  • Structured — a summary, strengths, current focus and development priorities.
  • Based on recorded evidence — sessions, attendance and assessments sit beside it.
  • Intentionally published — a family sees a review only when the coach publishes it.
  • Under controlled permissions — not every note or assessment is shared.

A Development Review is a coach's attestation. It is not independent verification.

Provenance

Recorded, coach-attested and independently verified are different things.

Athlytica shows the state of every entry, and the state is a word, not a colour.

Recorded

Captured with its source, session, time and author.

Coach-attested

A named coach, affiliated to a named organisation, stands behind it.

Independently verified

Checked by someone other than the recorder. Not currently claimed.

Athlytica does not currently claim independent verification where none exists.
