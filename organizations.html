01 — The session loop

Start the record. Coach the session. Finish the entry.

Open the session when coaching starts, coach normally, then complete the entry before leaving. That is the whole workflow.

01

Open the session

Pick the organisation and discipline. A server-generated reference is issued and the roster freezes at that moment.

02

Attendance

Marked during the session or immediately after. Unmarked is never silently read as absent — a blank is a blank.

03

Assessment

Against the selected versioned operator protocol, where one applies.

04

Coach note

What the athlete should work on next, in the coach's own words, attributed to them.

05

Review

What was captured and what is still blank, shown before anything closes.

06

Finish

Terminal. A closed session takes no new evidence, which is what makes the register worth reading later.

02 — What it gives an organisation

A register that survives a change of coach.

Athletes and coaches

Authorised records can be connected while each coach attests under their own name.

Sessions and rosters

Frozen when the session starts, so a register cannot be rewritten afterwards.

Independent practice

You do not need an academy behind you — declare a practice and record under it.

More than one discipline

Each stays in its own context. Nothing is averaged across sports.

Connected records, separate contexts

Designed to reduce duplicate identities. Suspected duplicates are reviewed and corrected with an audit trail.

Reporting to families

Guardian access is invitation-only and under controlled validation. It starts with the participating organisation.

03 — Pilot & trial

Thirty days, because thirty days is what it takes to mean anything.

A seven-day pilot can demonstrate the session loop but cannot produce a single comparable measure. Thirty days spans a real training block.

Bring your athletes

Your roster, your disciplines, your coaching.

Run real sessions

Not a sandbox. The work you were doing anyway.

Record the work

Attendance, assessment and notes, attributed to the coach who made them.

Invite families

Where guardian access applies, and subject to recorded authority.

See the record build

At the end you have a register you can read, or you have learned the model does not fit.

A conversation first

No card and no account are created by enquiring. Setup and data onboarding are confirmed with your organisation.

A pilot never promises scouting placement, scholarships or verified status. Pilot availability depends on product, privacy and protocol readiness, and organisation access is configured as part of the controlled pilot.
